# Wealth Legacy Alliance — 15-Minute Video Production Script

## 1. Primary Video Title

**Stop Starting Over: Build One Network, One System, One Legacy**

## 2. Alternative Titles

1. **Wealth Legacy Alliance: A Better Way to Build**
2. **One Community. Many Possibilities. A Lasting Legacy.**
3. **Turn Business Relationships Into a Long-Term Legacy**

## 3. Opening Title Card

**What if every relationship you build could remain valuable—beyond one product, one company, or one opportunity?**

## 4. Recommended Visual Style and Color Direction

- **Style:** Premium corporate documentary blended with clean technology animation.
- **Palette:** Deep navy `#071A33`, royal blue `#175CD3`, warm gold `#D7A93B`, white, and soft gray.
- **Typography:** Modern sans-serif; bold headings, clean body copy, generous spacing.
- **Footage:** Authentic entrepreneurs and families in natural environments. Avoid staged celebrations and stereotypical direct-sales imagery.
- **Graphics:** Thin gold connection lines, softly illuminated network nodes, restrained interface animation, and subtle depth.
- **Music:** Begin reflective and minimal, build into confident cinematic corporate music, then resolve warmly.
